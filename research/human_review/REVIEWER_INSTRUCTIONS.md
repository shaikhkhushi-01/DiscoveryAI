# Reviewer instructions — NLP corpus pilot

## 1. Study scope
The review concerns NLP and language-model research, including computational linguistics; NLP-oriented information retrieval, question answering and RAG; evaluation; robustness/safety; linguistic analysis; and reproducibility. Publication window: 2018-01-01 through 2025-12-31.

Use only assigned study records. The coordinator supplies opaque IDs and approved metadata or rights-cleared full text. Do not search for another reviewer's decisions.

## 2. Independence and blinding
- Complete decisions independently; do not discuss individual records with the other reviewer.
- Do not view the other reviewer's spreadsheet or comments before both first-pass files are submitted and locked.
- Do not alter a locked first-pass file. Corrections require a dated amendment.
- Do not guess missing information. Use UNCERTAIN or MISSING_INFO.
- Declare conflicts to the coordinator before review. Keep personal details out of repository files.

## 3. Title/abstract scope decision
Choose exactly one:
- INCLUDE: clearly indicates an in-scope NLP research contribution and no clear exclusion.
- EXCLUDE: clearly outside NLP/language-model scope, outside the publication window, not a full research paper, or otherwise explicitly excluded by protocol.
- UNCERTAIN: information is insufficient or ambiguous; requires full-text or adjudicator review.
- MISSING_INFO: title/abstract is absent or unusable.

Do not use rights/access as a proxy for scientific scope. Rights review is separate. A paper may be in-scope but not legally usable for full-text mining.

## 4. Full-text rights decision
Review only rights evidence supplied by the coordinator. Choose:
- APPROVED: explicit recorded evidence establishes permission for the specific intended full-text processing and storage/use.
- NOT_APPROVED: evidence explicitly disallows the intended activity or permission is absent after review.
- UNCERTAIN: license/terms/evidence are ambiguous or require legal/institutional advice.
- NOT_ASSESSED: no rights review has occurred.

A reachable URL, repository availability, OpenAlex has_fulltext, or a license label without confirming scope is insufficient. Record evidence URL, license/permission identifier, reviewer, date, and precise permitted activity. Escalate ambiguity; do not provide legal advice. Until approved, full-text processing remains prohibited for this workflow.

## 5. Day 3 paper-level annotation
Annotate only rights-cleared material and fields in the supplied annotation protocol. Unit: work/version family, not an arbitrary PDF chunk. Use traceable page/section/paragraph or document/chunk references supplied by the coordinator. Summarize in your own words; avoid copying substantial copyrighted passages.

Taxonomy: MODEL_METHOD, DATA_RESOURCE, EVALUATION, ROBUSTNESS_SAFETY, RETRIEVAL_QA_RAG, LINGUISTIC_ANALYSIS, REPRODUCIBILITY, OTHER_NLP, OUT_OF_SCOPE.

Do not invent methods, results, limitations, or evidence. Use NOT_REPORTED for absent information and UNCERTAIN where interpretation is unclear.

## 6. Submit and lock
Return the completed file through the agreed restricted channel. Include reviewer code, protocol/schema version, completion timestamp, and file checksum. Coordinator records receipt and locks the file before revealing comparisons. Do not upload private identity or conflict data to the public repository.

## 7. Questions and deviations
Ask the coordinator about ambiguous protocol rules before discussing a specific paper with the other reviewer. Record deviations, reason, date, and affected study IDs. Do not silently change the protocol mid-pilot.
